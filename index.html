Protection

Six protections that work on the same signal

Each one feeds the others. A suspicious link raises the risk score of the next sign-in, and a trusted device lowers it.

Credential Shield

Compares login velocity, device reuse and known-breach pairs, then challenges or blocks before an account is touched.

Velocity · reuse · breach pairs

Phishing Radar

Scans links at click time, so a page that turned malicious after delivery is still caught. Reads SPF, DKIM and DMARC results too.

SPF · DKIM · DMARC aware

Device Trust

Binds each session to a key held on the device. A stolen cookie replayed from another machine is worthless.

Device-bound sessions

Breach Watch

Checks your addresses against known breaches using k-anonymity, so the full hash of your password never leaves your device.

k-anonymity lookups

Passkey Vault

Create and sync passkeys, with authenticator-app codes and hardware keys as fallbacks when a site is not ready.

FIDO2 · WebAuthn · TOTP (RFC 6238)

When something trips, an analyst opens the case with the evidence attached and walks you through containment.

Evidence-first response

    Questions

    Straight answers

    Does Ironveil see my passwords?

    No. Vault contents are encrypted on your device before they sync, and the password check on this page runs entirely in your browser.

    What happens when a sign-in looks risky?

    Low risk passes without a prompt. Medium risk asks for a passkey or a one-time code. High risk is blocked and logged with the evidence that triggered it.

    Do I have to replace my current two-step sign-in?

    No. Ironveil works alongside authenticator apps, text-message codes and hardware keys, and suggests passkeys for accounts that support them.

    Where do the addresses in the live feed come from?

    The feed is sample data. Its addresses sit in ranges reserved for documentation (192.0.2.0/24, 198.51.100.0/24 and 203.0.113.0/24), so none of them belong to a real network.

    Can I run it for a team?

    Yes. The Business plan adds single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, policy controls and exportable audit logs.
